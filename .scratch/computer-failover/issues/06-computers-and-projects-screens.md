# 06 — Computers and Projects screens

**Status:** ready-for-agent
**Blocked by:** 03

Spec: `../spec.md`, Surfaces (HTTP, Web). Built in auto design mode, then animation mode.

Two tabs replace three. Computers: Your computers with drag and keyboard reorder, each computer's runs at once, live
"n of m running", chat folder and existing facts, and the defaults (model, start-in, placement, clone automatically).
Projects: each project follows your computers or names its own list, shows per computer whether it has the checkout,
with **Add to <computer>**. Queue rows show every computer's reason. `/settings/pairing/defaults` 404s.

Done when: verified at 768, 1024 and 1440px against a demo database copy with two computers, one offline.
