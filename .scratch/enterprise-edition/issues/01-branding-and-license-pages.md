# How the License and Branding settings look

Type: prototype
Status: resolved
Blocked by: None — can start immediately

## Question

What do the instance Owner's License and Branding pages in Settings look like, and what does a branded
instance look like with a client's name, logo, accent and an imported theme? Rough variants of both pages
and a branded sign-in page and board, for the owner to pick from at 768, 1024 and 1440 px in light and
dark. The pick shapes NEXUL-122 and the settings part of NEXUL-120.

## Answer

One Branding card under License on Settings → Instance, in the same settings grammar as the cards around it: hairline rows for Product name, Logo, Accent, Dark theme and Light theme, one Save in the footer for the name and accent, and the logo and each imported theme saving the moment the file is chosen. It won over the same rows split into an Identity card and a Colors card (shot side by side at 1024 px, light and dark): both match the design language row for row, and one card has one footer and one hint instead of two, so the tie went to fewer elements. The License card gains a row per feature its key lists, each with a status dot, so a feature's own card and its row there say the same thing; without a key the Branding card keeps what is saved, shows it greyed under a warning that names License, and the logo shows as "Saved". A branded instance changes no structure: the sidebar shows the logo and name (Nexul's Beta pill steps aside), sign-in sets the name in its headline with the logo in the corner mark and a small "Runs on Nexul" line under the card, and the board takes the accent, or the imported theme's colors when there is one. Shots at 768, 1024 and 1440 px in light and dark covered a violet imported theme, a long name with a wide logo and a yellow accent that had to be darkened, and the locked state.
